## Overview

Led the design, simulation, layout, assembly, and experimental validation of a low-noise balanced photodetector for homodyne detection in quantum optics experiments.

## What I worked on

- Designed the detector circuit in LTSpice
- Built the PCB in EasyEDA
- Built a free-space interferometer and measured gain, bandwidth, and noise performance
- Integrated the detector into an optical setup for characterization

## Outcome

Achieved SNRs of approximately 25 dB (high-gain) and 12 dB (low-gain), at under $20 in components per detector versus roughly $2,000 for a commercial balanced detector.
