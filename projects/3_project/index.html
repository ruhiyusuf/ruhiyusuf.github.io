Balanced Homodyne Photodetector PCB

Designed and fabricated a low-noise balanced photodetector for homodyne detection in quantum optics experiments, focusing on analog circuit design, PCB layout, and optical testing.

Overview

Designed and fabricated a low-noise balanced photodetector for homodyne detection in quantum optics experiments. This project focused on analog circuit design, PCB layout, and experimental testing for optical measurement systems.

What I worked on

  • Designed the detector circuit in LTSpice
  • Built the PCB in EasyEDA
  • Tested gain, bandwidth, and noise performance
  • Integrated the detector into an optical setup for characterization
Left: fabricated balanced photodetector PCB. Right: optical setup used for detector testing and characterization.

Outcome

This work explored whether a significantly lower-cost detector could still provide useful performance for balanced homodyne detection and related optical sensing applications.
